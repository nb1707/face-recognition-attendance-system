Metric Explanation

False Accept: An unknown person is incorrectly recognized as a registered user.

False Reject: A registered person is rejected as unknown, no face, or multiple faces.

Wrong Identity: A registered person is recognized as a different registered person.

Known Identity Accuracy: Percentage of registered-person images assigned to the correct identity.
